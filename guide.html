Book a Ticket

Complete your booking in a few steps

1

Search your route

Select From, To and journey date from the homepage.

2

Choose your bus

Open the available bus and check fare, timing, route and amenities.

3

Select pickup, drop and seats

Choose your boarding point, dropping point and available seats.

4

Add passenger details

Enter passenger name, age, gender and contact number correctly.

5

Pay and confirm

Complete payment securely. Your ticket will appear in Profile / My Trips.

Note: You can book up to 6 seats at a time.

Cancel a Ticket

Cancel from your journey details

1

Open Profile

Go to Profile and find your active booking.

2

Check refund preview

Tap Cancel and review the refund amount before confirming.

3

Confirm cancellation

Once confirmed, your seats will be released and refund will be processed.

Reschedule Journey

Change your journey date once

1

Open your ticket

Go to Profile and select the booking you want to reschedule.

2

Choose new date and trip

Select an available date and bus trip for your new journey.

3

Select same number of seats

You must choose the same number of seats as the original booking.

4

Confirm reschedule

Your old seats will be released and new seats will be booked.

Important: Reschedule is allowed only once and only up to 12 hours before departure.

Boarding Rules

Important information before travel

Reach 15 minutes early

Please report at your pickup point before departure time.

M-Ticket accepted

You can show your ticket on mobile during boarding.

Carry valid ID proof

Government ID may be required for passenger verification.

No waiting at pickup

The bus may not wait if passenger is late at the boarding point.

Luggage & Conduct

Travel rules for all passengers

Two luggage pieces free

Extra or heavy luggage may be chargeable.

Above 15kg may be charged

Please avoid carrying oversized luggage.

Pets are not allowed

Animals are not permitted inside the bus.

Liquor is prohibited

Carrying or consuming liquor inside the bus is not allowed.
