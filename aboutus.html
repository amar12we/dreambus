Passenger Policies

  • Passengers should report at the pickup point at least 15 minutes before departure.
  • Digital ticket or M-Ticket is valid for boarding.
  • Valid Government ID is required during boarding.
  • Cancellation refund is calculated based on time left before departure.
  • Refunds may take 5–6 working days to reflect in the original payment method.
  • Reschedule is allowed only once and only up to 12 hours before departure.
  • Two pieces of luggage are allowed. Extra luggage above 15 kg may be chargeable.
  • Pets, liquor and restricted items are not allowed inside the bus.
  • The bus will not wait for late passengers at pickup points.
