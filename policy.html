Dreamliner Booking Policy

Smooth Journeys, Superior Comfort

Please read the booking, cancellation, safety and travel policies carefully before confirming your Dreamliner ticket.

1. Female Passenger Safety Policy

Dreamliner follows a strict adjacent-seat safety rule to ensure a safer and more comfortable journey for female passengers.

Mandatory Rule: A seat directly adjacent to a female passenger cannot be booked by a male passenger from a separate booking transaction.
  • Automated Restriction: The system automatically restricts male passenger selection next to an already booked female passenger seat.
  • Co-Traveller Exception: This rule does not apply when both seats are booked in the same transaction, such as family or couple bookings.
  • Incorrect Information: If wrong gender details are provided to bypass the rule, the operator may reassign or cancel the seat as required.
  • Operator Authority: Final seat adjustment rights remain with the bus operator for passenger safety and operational reasons.

2. Booking Rules

  • Tickets are valid only for the selected travel date, route, trip and time.
  • Tickets are non-transferable and cannot be used by another passenger.
  • Passenger name, age, gender and phone number must be entered correctly during booking.
  • Passengers must carry a valid Government ID such as Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving Licence or Passport.
  • Dreamliner is not responsible for issues caused by incorrect passenger, pickup or drop details entered during booking.

3. Boarding Policy

  • Digital ticket or M-Ticket is valid for boarding.
  • Passengers must report at the selected pickup point at least 15 minutes before departure.
  • The bus will not wait for late passengers.
  • Passengers must board only from the selected pickup point shown on the ticket.
  • Government ID may be checked before boarding.

4. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Refund amount is calculated based on the time left before scheduled departure.

Cancellation Time Deduction Refund
More than 48 hours before departure 10% 90%
24 to 48 hours before departure 20% 80%
12 to 24 hours before departure 50% 50%
Less than 12 hours before departure 100% No refund
Refunds may take 5–6 working days to reflect in the original payment method, depending on the bank or payment provider.

5. Reschedule Policy

  • Reschedule is allowed only once per booking.
  • Reschedule is available up to 12 hours before departure.
  • Reschedule is not allowed within 12 hours of departure.
  • Passenger count must remain the same while rescheduling.
  • Coupons or discounts may not be applicable during reschedule.
  • Once rescheduled, the ticket cannot be rescheduled again.

6. Luggage Policy

  • Each passenger is allowed up to 2 pieces of luggage.
  • Extra luggage or luggage above 15 kg may be chargeable.
  • Passengers are responsible for their personal belongings.
  • Dreamliner is not responsible for loss of valuables kept in luggage.

7. Restricted Items

  • Pets are not allowed inside the bus.
  • Liquor and illegal substances are strictly prohibited.
  • Dangerous, inflammable or restricted goods are not allowed.
  • Passengers causing disturbance may be denied boarding or removed from the journey.

8. GST, Fare & Service Fees

  • GST and applicable charges are shown clearly during checkout.
  • The final payable amount is displayed before payment confirmation.
  • Convenience fees, payment gateway charges or service charges may be non-refundable.
  • Fare may vary based on route, date, trip, seat availability and offers.

9. Coupons & Offers

  • Coupons are valid only for eligible users and eligible bookings.
  • Some coupons may be limited to one-time use per user.
  • Coupon benefits may change, pause or expire without prior notice.
  • Coupons are not applicable after booking confirmation.
  • Coupons may not be available during reschedule bookings.

10. Operator Rights

  • Dreamliner may change bus, seat or schedule due to operational reasons.
  • Seat reassignment may be done for safety, technical or management reasons.
  • In case of unavoidable delay, passengers will be informed through available contact details.
  • Dreamliner reserves the right to refuse travel in case of misconduct, invalid ticket or incorrect passenger details.

11. Important Travel Notice

  • Passengers should check pickup point, departure time and journey date before travel.
  • Passengers should keep their phone reachable for trip-related communication.
  • Dreamliner is not responsible for missed buses due to passenger delay.
  • By booking a ticket, the passenger agrees to all Dreamliner booking policies.
